# Sovereign share, paired presentation

Closing pass, item 2. No new analysis. This restates the existing numbers from
`framework/labor_backing/item3_sovereign_robustness.csv` and `sensitivity.csv` in the form the
paper should use.

## The one-step rule IS the first-round versus second-round distinction

The one-step rule says a claim's labour backing is the labour share of the cash flow that
**directly** services it, one step. Corporate bonds, corporate and noncorporate business
loans, non-multifamily commercial mortgages and corporate equity are therefore zero by rule:
they are serviced from **business revenue**, and labour reaches them only through **spending**.

That is not an arbitrary convention. **It is the same first-round versus second-round boundary
the rest of the paper is built on.** The displacement engine measures first-round losses with
house prices, consumer demand and business revenue held fixed; the second-round module then
measures the demand channel separately and finds it is nine tenths of the bank channel. The
one-step rule draws the identical line on the stock side that the engine draws on the flow
side.

## The pair, reported side by side

| | what it measures | **federal share** |
|---|---|---|
| **FIRST ROUND: claims serviced DIRECTLY from labour income** | mortgages, rent, consumer credit, student loans, Treasury and municipal debt | **0.794**, agreed range **0.778 to 0.804** |
| **INCLUDING SECOND ROUND: indirectly serviced claims added** | business-revenue-serviced claims at the measured indirect labour share, that is, business revenue funded by wage-financed spending | **0.452** |

**Both numbers are correct and they measure different objects.** The first is the share of the
directly wage-backed claim stock that the federal government holds, guarantees or owes. The
second is that share once the claim stock is widened to everything labour income reaches at
one further remove.

**The pair mirrors the paper's structure, and that is why it is reported as a pair rather than
as a headline plus a caveat:**

> **The state is the first-round holder. Private balance sheets are the second-round holders.**

The sovereign concentration is a statement about **direct** exposure. Widening to indirect
exposure does not weaken the finding; it relocates it, because the claims added are held by
banks, insurers, pensions, foreign investors and households rather than by the state. The fall
from 0.794 to 0.452 is itself the measurement of that relocation.

The labour backing **ratio** moves the other way on the same call, from **0.270** to
**0.475**, because widening the numerator adds labour-backed claims without adding to the
total. The two moves are the same accounting fact seen from the two ends.

## The other three structural calls, with their effect

| call | federal share | move from 0.794 |
|---|---|---|
| **obligor leg EXCLUDED** (held or guaranteed only, the state's own debt not counted) | **0.321** | **-59.5 pct** |
| **agency pools NOT federal** (the guarantee ignored, investors bear it) | **0.587** | **-26.1 pct** |
| **commercial mortgage treated as rent-serviced** rather than zero | **0.741** | **-6.6 pct** |

The first two are definitional questions about what "sovereign exposure" means, not
measurement uncertainty, and each has a defensible reading:

- **Obligor leg excluded** answers a narrower question: what does the state hold as an
  *investor or guarantor*, setting aside what it owes. 0.321.
- **Agency pools not federal** tests the guarantee. If the Enterprise guarantee is treated as
  non-binding, the share falls to 0.587. Item 1 of this session bears on it directly: the
  Enterprises retain 85 to 89 percent of an agency loss at small doses and the statutory
  Treasury trigger is not reached, so the guarantee is neither free nor called.
- **Commercial mortgage** is a one-step-rule question in miniature, and it is grouped with the
  structural calls for that reason.

## What does NOT move it

**No measurement call moves the sovereign share by more than 1.3 percent.** The entire
Treasury definitional question, which drove 83.7 percent of the replication gap, is worth
0.009. Whether the central bank counts as federal moves it by **exactly zero**, because
Federal Reserve Treasury holdings enter the held leg and the overlap in equal measure.

**The sentence for the paper:** the sovereign share is a robust measurement under a stated
accounting convention; the convention is the first-round boundary the whole paper uses; and
both sides of it are reported.

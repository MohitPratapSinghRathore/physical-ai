# Pre-registration: order of stress across displacement regimes

Registered **before** running the frontier, the sudden-shock module or the order-of-stress
engine. Written 2026-09-19. Owner-specified.

## Regimes

| Regime | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Slow** | Annual displacement flow at or below the speed limit from A56 and its destination-pool variants |
| **Fast** | Above the speed limit but with implied unemployment still inside the observed DWS range (at or below 9.8 percent) |
| **Sudden** | Implied unemployment outside the observed range. The fitted DWS relationships do not apply and results are reported as bands, labelled SCENARIO |

## The registered expectation

> In the **slow** regime only the public budget fails.
>
> In the **fast** regime household distress and rent arrears follow.
>
> In the **sudden** regime the double trigger (payment shortfall AND negative equity)
> reaches bank-held mortgages in cognitive-heavy metros.

## What would count as refuting it

- Slow regime: any balance sheet other than the public budget crossing its stated
  materiality threshold.
- Fast regime: rent arrears NOT preceding mortgage losses, or household distress remaining
  below threshold while another sheet crosses.
- Sudden regime: the double trigger concentrating in embodied-heavy rather than
  cognitive-heavy metros, or agency rather than bank-held portfolios taking the larger share.
- Any regime: an ordering that is not monotone in displacement, or one that reverses between
  the two cognitive indices.

## Standing constraints on how this is reported

- Every conversion factor carries a source and a range; the ordering's robustness to those
  ranges is reported.
- Materiality thresholds are stated before the result, not chosen to fit it.
- Inside the observed data range, estimates. Outside it, bands, labelled.
- The result is reported as the engine gives it, including if it contradicts the above.

## Prior results that bear on this and could bias the reading

- A41: at 10 percent displacement the household channel is small (+2.35pp of DSTI-50
  crossings against a 10.08 percent baseline).
- A42: share-based attribution overstates distress-relevant obligations about fivefold.
- A56: the household channel at 10 percent over 10 years is smaller still (u = 4.84).
- A57 and A58: the public budget condition already fails in 2026 at zero AI displacement.

**A57 and A58 make the slow-regime half of the prediction nearly tautological**: the public
budget is already failing before any displacement. That is registered here so the result is
not presented as a discovery. The informative content of the prediction is the ORDER of the
other two sheets and the metro composition in the sudden regime.

## Amendment, 2026-09-19, owner decision

Renamed from "order of failure" to "order of stress". **The table must allow the outcome that NO balance sheet reaches its materiality threshold.** The registered expectation below is a prediction about ordering CONDITIONAL on any sheet crossing; "none crosses" is a permitted and reportable result, and given A41 and A56 it is a live possibility for the slow regime on every sheet except the public budget.

Regimes are also redefined per the same decision: INSIDE the observed data range, OUTSIDE it, and a BOUNDARY BAND reported as such, replacing the earlier SLOW/FAST/SUDDEN split whose middle band turned out to be nearly empty (A63).

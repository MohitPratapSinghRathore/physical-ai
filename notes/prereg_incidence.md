# Pre-registration: who bears the loss under three incidence cases

Registered **before** running the household stress engine on the incidence cases. Written
2026-09-19. Owner-specified expectation, recorded verbatim in substance.

## Design

Same TOTAL employment loss, delivered three ways:

| Case | Delivery | alpha |
|---|---|---|
| (a) | Laid-off incumbents | 0 |
| (b) | Never-hired entrants, attrition absorption | 1 |
| (c) | Sourced mix from BLS separation rates | implied |

For case (b) the balance sheets are those of households headed by or containing people aged
22 to 29 in ACS PUMS and SIPP: debt types, rent against own, co-residence with parents,
student loans.

Reported for each: DSTI crossings, runway, and mortgage, auto, rent and student loan
exposure, plus the fiscal loss.

## The registered expectation

> Attrition absorption leaves the FISCAL loss unchanged, cuts mortgage and auto stress
> sharply, and shifts stress to rent, student loans and delayed household formation.

## What would refute it

- Fiscal loss differing materially between (a) and (b).
- Mortgage or auto stress NOT falling under (b).
- Rent or student loan exposure NOT rising under (b).
- The ordering reversing between ACS and SIPP, or between the two cognitive indices.

## A correction to the previous session that this pre-registration exists to prevent

A64 and A65 reported that attrition absorption is "exactly neutral" for prime-age E/P,
unemployment, wage income and the speed limit. **That neutrality is an ACCOUNTING PROPERTY of
treating laid-off incumbents and never-hired entrants symmetrically in an aggregate
stock-flow model. It is not an empirical finding about equivalent harm.** The two groups hold
completely different balance sheets, and the aggregate labour-market identity is silent about
that. This item exists precisely because the aggregate neutrality says nothing about
incidence, and the write-up must say so wherever the neutrality is mentioned.

## Prior results that bear on this

- A41: cognitive exposure concentrates in households with income to spare; embodied in
  households without it.
- A44: cognitive households are likelier to hold a mortgage; conditional on holding one,
  embodied households carry more service per dollar earned.
- A47: non-working households hold 16.0 percent of mortgage service and 27.3 percent of rent.
- A38 and A47: renters skew to lower-wage and younger households.

These make the direction of the expectation plausible in advance, which is why it is
registered rather than discovered.

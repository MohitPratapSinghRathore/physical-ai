\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

Every limitation below is carried in the released limitations file and the numbering follows it.

\paragraph{L1. Off-balance-sheet and hardware-backed AI financing is unmeasured.} The AI side is
built from nine registrants' on-balance-sheet facts. Off-balance-sheet vehicles, which
\citet{BIS2026} identifies as dominant in data center financing, contribute nothing to it, and
neither does hardware-backed lending or securitization. Every level on that side is a lower bound
and every federal share of it an upper bound, which is why Sections~\ref{sec:aiside}
and~\ref{sec:bust} report it as bounds rather than as a point.

\paragraph{L2. Capital gains inside federal receipts are not separated.} Realized gains sit inside
the base we split by the wage share of adjusted gross income, so the labor-linked share overstates
in years with large realizations and understates in years without. The band is carried throughout
and the federal exposure share moves by under one percent across it; the fiscal magnitudes are
more sensitive and we have not bounded that.

\paragraph{L3. The novelty of the accounts is ``none located'', not ``none exists''.} No
systematic search with a stated protocol has been run for this specific question. The related-work
audit did locate prior work on the fiscal mechanism and that priority claim was retired; the same
could happen here.

\paragraph{L4. The holder split leaves an unidentified remainder.} Three of the four shares in the
mortgage holder split are read from a publisher and the fourth is an arithmetic remainder, treated
as private in full. That is conservative for our claim, since any federal fraction inside it would
raise the federal share, but we have not identified what is in it, and the AI side has a remainder
of the same kind.

\paragraph{L5. The effective labor tax rate does not vary across the wage distribution.} It is a
single economy-wide rate, and because displacement is concentrated away from the top of the
distribution a flat rate probably overstates the revenue loss from displacing low-paid workers and
understates it from displacing high-paid ones. We have not signed or bounded the net effect.

\paragraph{L14. The base of the taxable shareholder share decides how much of the parameter space
clears the threshold.} Ours is equity in taxable accounts over all US equity outstanding; the
Congressional Research Service uses the same numerator over domestically held stock alone. Ours is
the right base for a rate on a dollar of US surplus whoever holds it, but the choice is
load-bearing. On our base the share of the stated grid in which the condition closes, uniformly
weighted and not a probability, is \result{PassShareOursEasy} percent against the easier labor
tax reading and \result{PassShareOursHard} percent against the harder; on the domestic-holder
base it is \result{PassShareCRSEasy} and \result{PassShareCRSHard} percent, with the central rate
rising to \result{TauKCRSCentral} percent. The central case falls short on either base, and the corners
reach further than the pass shares suggest: the analytic maximum on our base,
\result{AnalyticMaxOurs} percent, exceeds the easier required rate, which is why the statement
this paper makes is the share of the space rather than a claim about every corner of it.

\paragraph{What a rebuild does not establish.} The independent computational rebuilds described
in Section~\ref{sec:rebuild} and Appendix~\ref{app:rebuild} establish that the stated inputs and
the stated construction produce the stated numbers. They do not establish that the constructions
are the right ones, or that the economic assumptions behind them hold. The rebuild of a scenario
is a rebuild of arithmetic.

\paragraph{Method limitations.} Four are properties of the method rather than of a datum: the
extrapolation boundary above about a quarter of the wage bill, the first-round scope of the credit
engine, the procedural blind in the rebuild protocol, and the treatment of dividends to foreign
holders as untaxed at the owner level in the base construction, whose effect is sized in
Section~\ref{sec:fiscal} and does not close the condition. Appendix~\ref{app:detail} states each
in full.

Five quantities the accounts can produce are not reported here, and Appendix
Table~\ref{tab:removed} lists each with its reason.

\paragraph{The household counterpart.} The specification, the deviations log and both
results files behind Section~ef{sec:displacement}'s household counterpart, the second of
which is post hoc and labeled as such, are archived with the replication package.

\paragraph{What the accounts are not.} We tested, under a pre-registered design fixed before
any outcome was opened, whether labor backing measured before the 2014 to 2016 oil price collapse
predicts subsequent household-loan charge-offs across about 5,900 US banks beyond conventional
measures. It does not. At bank level the accounts' measure is about nine tenths reproducible from
public loan shares and local wage shares, the debtor-specific component that is distinctive to the
accounts had no predictive content, and neither it nor the public rival improved out-of-sample
prediction in held-out states. The design's own pre-shock placebo failed under the standard errors
we had named as governing, so the episode supports no causal reading in either direction. In that
episode the local wage shock predicted business-loan losses strongly and household-loan losses not
at all. We therefore present labor backing accounts as aggregate accounting of where wage
dependence sits, not as an institution-level risk measure, and we note that this one historical
episode offers no evidence for a strong household-credit channel at bank level. The
pre-registration, amendments, deviations log and results are archived with the replication
package, in the validation pilot record described in the data availability statement.

\paragraph{Avenues for further work.} Each limitation above has a corresponding avenue, set out in Appendix~\ref{app:detail}: measuring the off-balance-sheet side directly, separating realized gains inside the tax base, running a systematic novelty search, identifying the holder remainder, using labor tax rates by position in the income distribution, constructing both bases for the taxable-holder share, extending the reemployment relationship beyond its observed range, letting house prices and spending move with displacement, and running a rebuild with an enforced blind.

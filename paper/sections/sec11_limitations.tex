\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

Every limitation below is carried in the released limitations file, and the numbering here follows
it so that a reader can move between the two. Each is paired one to one with an avenue of future
research in Section~\ref{sec:future}.

\paragraph{L1. Off-balance-sheet and hardware-backed AI financing is unmeasured.} The AI side is
built from nine registrants' on-balance-sheet facts. Off-balance-sheet vehicles, which the Bank
for International Settlements identifies as dominant in data centre financing, contribute nothing
to it, and neither does hardware-backed lending or data centre securitization. Every level on the
AI side is therefore a lower bound and every federal share of it an upper bound. We do not know the
sign of the error on the gap between the two sides, only that the gap is measured against a side we
can bound from below.

\paragraph{L2. Capital gains inside federal receipts are not separated.} The labor-linked share of
federal receipts splits the individual income tax by the wage share of adjusted gross income, and
realized gains sit inside that base. The share is therefore an overstatement in years with large
realizations and an understatement in years without. The band is carried throughout and the federal
exposure share moves by under one percent across it, so the headline is not sensitive to this. The
fiscal magnitudes are more sensitive and we have not bounded that.

\paragraph{L3. The novelty of the accounts is ``none located'', not ``none exists''.} No systematic
search with a stated protocol, databases, query strings and counts at each stage has been run for
the specific question of whether anyone has constructed this object before. The related-work audit
did locate prior work on the fiscal mechanism, and that priority claim was retired. The same could
happen here.

\paragraph{L4. The holder split leaves an unidentified remainder.} Three of the four shares in the
mortgage holder split are read from a publisher and the fourth is an arithmetic remainder, treated
as private in full. That is the conservative choice for our claim, since any federal fraction
inside it would raise the federal share rather than lower it, but we have not identified what is
in it. A remainder of the same kind appears on the AI side.

\paragraph{L5. The effective labor tax rate does not vary across the wage distribution.} It is a
single economy-wide rate, built from federal taxes over wages and salaries. Because displacement is
not uniform across the distribution, and because the incidence results show it concentrated away
from the top, a flat rate probably overstates the revenue loss from displacing low-paid workers and
understates it from displacing high-paid ones. We have not signed or bounded the net effect.

\paragraph{L14. The base of the taxable shareholder share decides how stable the capital tax
verdict is.} Our share is equity in taxable accounts over all US equity outstanding, foreign
holders included. The Congressional Research Service uses the same numerator over domestically
held stock alone. Ours is the right base for a rate on a dollar of US surplus whoever holds it,
because a foreign holder bears close to no US tax at the owner level and that dollar is still in
the surplus, but the choice is load-bearing: on the other base the central rate rises to
\result{TauKCRSCentral} percent, the share of the parameter space that closes the condition rises
to \result{TauKCRSPassShare} percent against the easier labor tax reading, and the analytic
maximum clears the harder threshold. The statement that no corner of the space closes the
condition holds only on our base, and it carries that qualification wherever it appears. The
centre fails under both rent readings on either base.

\paragraph{Method limitations.} Four more are properties of the method rather than of a parameter.
The \emph{extrapolation boundary} described in Section~\ref{sec:displacement}: above about a
quarter of the wage bill displaced there are no point estimates, only bands, because the
reemployment fit leaves the range of the data. That is a limit of the specification and not a fact
about the economy. The \emph{first-round scope} of the credit engine, which holds house prices,
spending, business revenue and the employment of everyone not displaced fixed, so every credit
loss is a floor rather than an estimate. The \emph{procedural blind} in the replication protocol,
described in Section~\ref{sec:data}, which is why this paper says quantities were independently
rebuilt under a reported blind protocol and never that they were independently verified. And
\emph{foreign withholding}: dividends to foreign holders bear US withholding tax at treaty rates
and we treat them as untaxed at the owner level, which biases the assembled capital tax rate
downward by an amount we have not quantified. That last one runs against our own finding, which is
why it appears here and in Section~\ref{sec:fiscal} rather than only in a note.

\section{Avenues of future research}
\label{sec:future}

Each item below addresses the limitation with the same number.

\paragraph{FR1.} Build the off-balance-sheet side of AI financing directly, from securitization
prospectuses, vehicle-level filings and supervisory data on lending secured against computing
hardware, so that the AI side can be measured rather than bounded from below and the gap between
the two sides can be stated with a two-sided error.

\paragraph{FR2.} Separate realized capital gains from wage income inside the individual income tax
base using the published tax statistics by source and size of income, and re-run the fiscal
magnitudes on the separated series, so that the sensitivity we can currently only name is bounded.

\paragraph{FR3.} Run a systematic search with a published protocol for prior constructions of a
labor backing decomposition, reporting databases, query strings, dates and counts at each stage,
and publish the result whichever way it comes out.

\paragraph{FR4.} Identify the remainder in the mortgage holder split from loan-level or
servicer-level sources, so that the conservative treatment can be replaced by a measurement, and
apply the same treatment to the corresponding remainder on the AI side.

\paragraph{FR5.} Replace the single economy-wide labor tax rate with effective rates by position
in the income distribution, from the published distributional tax tables, and re-run the fiscal
condition with displacement weighted by where in the distribution it falls.

\paragraph{FR14.} Settle the base question for the taxable shareholder share, ideally by
constructing both bases from the same holder data and reporting the rate on each, so that a
reader can see which question each answers rather than having to choose between two published
numbers that are not comparable.

\paragraph{FR6.} Extend the reemployment relationship beyond the range of the observed data by
using a source that observes larger displacement episodes, whether historical, sectoral or
cross-country, so that the estimates above a quarter of the wage bill can become estimates rather
than bands.

\paragraph{FR7.} Close the first-round scope by letting house prices, spending and non-displaced
employment move together with displacement, which would turn the credit losses from a floor into
an estimate and would also let the state and local feedback loop named in
Section~\ref{sec:banks} be modelled rather than named.

\paragraph{FR8.} Run a replication with an enforced blind, on a separate machine with the sealed
values held by a third party, so that the protocol claim can be strengthened from independently
rebuilt to independently verified.

\paragraph{FR9.} Quantify the effect of withholding tax on dividends to foreign holders within the
assembled rate, using treaty rates weighted by the composition of foreign holdings, and report the
corrected rate even though the correction runs against the paper's own finding.

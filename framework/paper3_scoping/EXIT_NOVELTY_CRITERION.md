# Novelty criterion for the exit finding

**2026-09-26. Written before any search.**

## The two claims being checked

**Claim 1, historical.** The postwar fall in the state's share of wage-backed claims
(0.696 → 0.308, 1947–65) was a **substitution of private household debt for public**:
Treasury grew ×1.40 while household credit grew ×7.61 around it.

**Claim 2, forward.** That channel is **closed**, because the state now holds or guarantees
**0.5576** of household credit against **0.0204** in 1965, so household expansion no longer
dilutes the state's position.

## Kill criteria

**KILL-A.** A published work already shows the postwar decline in the government's share of
credit was driven by private household credit expansion, on a common denominator. Claim 1
is then not new and the paper rests on claim 2 alone.

**KILL-B.** A published work already argues that the growth of federal credit guarantees
forecloses the deleveraging channel, or that the state's guarantee book makes private credit
expansion non-dilutive to its position. Claim 2 is then not new either.

**KILL-A and KILL-B together → stop.** The finding is a restatement.

**NARROW.** Either half exists in its own literature but not joined on a common denominator,
and no forward-looking claim is drawn. **This is the expected outcome** and is not a kill;
the contribution is then the joining and the forward claim, at the lower-Q1 target already
recorded.

## The literatures expected to bear on it

Named now so that finding them is not mistaken for a clean check:

- Reinhart and Sbrancia, financial repression and postwar debt liquidation — the numerator.
- Postwar consumer and mortgage credit history — the household expansion.
- Krishnamurthy and Vissing-Jorgensen on government debt and private safe-asset supply —
  the closest theoretical relative, since it makes public and private claim supply substitutes.
- Jordà, Schularick and Taylor on the great leveraging.
- Debt sustainability and fiscal space.

## Standard of evidence

An absence of search hits is weak evidence and is recorded as an absence, not as novelty.
A near miss is reported with what it does and does not cover.

# Success criteria, stated before running anything

**Written 2026-09-21, before any regulatory source was read or any quantity computed.**

The instruction is to try option 1 and, if it does not work out, continue to 2, 3 and 4
until something important is found. **That is a search across four hypotheses.** Reporting
whichever of the four happens to look best, without saying how many were tried, is the
project-level version of the multiple-comparisons problem the validation pilot was built to
avoid.

**Discipline, fixed here:** each option gets a success criterion written *before* it is run.
Every option attempted is reported — passed or failed — with its criterion and its result.
The count of options tried appears in the final report whatever the outcome.

---

## Option 1 — the regulatory-treatment gap

**Claim to be tested:** capital regulation risk-weights claims by obligor and collateral
type, not by the income that services them, so the concentration of wage-backed claims in
one counterparty is invisible to the rules that exist to catch concentration.

**Works out if all three hold:**

1. **The risk weights are as expected and primary-sourced.** US Basel III standardised
   approach assigns materially different risk weights to claims with materially similar
   wage dependence — specifically, that direct US government obligations and agency
   exposures carry weights far below whole-loan residential mortgage and consumer exposures.
   Verified from 12 CFR 217 or the equivalent, not from recollection.
2. **The concentration rules exempt the concentrated counterparty.** The US
   single-counterparty credit limit and/or the Basel large-exposures framework exempts US
   government and agency exposures from the limits that would otherwise bind.
3. **The quantity is non-trivial.** The share of labour-backed claims carrying a risk weight
   at or below 20 percent, and the share exempt from concentration limits, is large enough
   to be worth a supervisor's attention — taken here as **above 50 percent**.

**Fails if:** the weights or exemptions are not as described; or the computed shares are
small; or the argument turns out to be well established in the supervisory literature, in
which case it is a restatement and not a contribution.

**Explicit limitation, acknowledged now:** this option produces a *framing* argument
supported by an arithmetic mapping. It is not a new measurement and not an estimate. Its
value rests on the mapping being correct and the point being unmade elsewhere.

---

## Option 2 — cross-country replication (euro area)

**Works out if:** the euro-area sovereign share of labour-backed claims can be built to the
same standard as the US number, and differs from 0.794 by enough to establish that the US
figure is a fact about US institutional structure rather than an accounting artefact.
**Threshold: a gap of 15 percentage points or more.**

**Fails if:** the ECB euro-area accounts do not support the holder decomposition; or the
euro-area share comes out within 15 points of the US, in which case the US number is not
distinctive.

---

## Option 3 — the household-side mechanism

**Works out if:** the finding that 47 percent of US corporate equity never reaches a
household, plus 25 percent locked in retirement vehicles, can be shown to be *absent* from
the papers that model the labour-to-capital shift (Mian–Straub–Sufi, Moll–Rachel–Restrepo,
Korinek–Stiglitz), and can be stated as a constraint those models do not impose.

**Fails if:** any of them already imposes it, or the point is already standard.

---

## Option 4 — descriptive aggregate co-movement

**Works out if:** the sovereign share of labour-backed claims co-moves with an identifiable
macro-financial series in a way that is interpretable and stable across subperiods.

**Fails if:** it does not, or the co-movement is an artefact of common trend.

**Hard limit, fixed now:** this option is **descriptive only**. It may not be reported as
predictive or causal, and it may not be presented as rehabilitating the validation pilot's
null. If it is the only option that "works", the honest report is that the exercise found a
correlation and nothing more.

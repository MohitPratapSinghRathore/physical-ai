# Novelty check for the inverted paper: criterion, fixed before searching

**2026-09-21. Written before any search was run.**

## The paper being scoped

> The sovereign **obligor** leg of the labour-backed claim stock is common to advanced
> economies and mechanical — a monotone transform of the government-to-household debt ratio
> (US 1.76 → 0.543; euro area 1.82 → 0.589). The **holder/guarantor** leg is not: **0.326 in
> the US against 0.095 in the euro area.** The US federal government has converted roughly a
> third of its wage-backed claim stock from private claims into sovereign-guaranteed claims
> through the agency and GSE system, at a scale no other advanced economy approaches. Those
> converted claims then receive 0–20 percent risk weights and concentration-limit exemptions.

## What would kill it

**KILL.** An existing paper already measures the federal guarantee share of US household
credit **and** compares it internationally. Then the measurement is done and the paper
collapses to the regulatory point of option 1 alone.

## What would narrow it

**NARROW-1.** The US agency/GSE guarantee share of mortgage credit is measured (it will be —
this is a large literature), but **not** as a share of household or wage-backed claims, and
**not** set against a comparator economy. Then the contribution is the framing and the
comparison, not the US measurement.

**NARROW-2.** Cross-country comparison of government involvement in housing finance exists
descriptively (it probably does), but without a claim-stock share or an income-source
dimension. Then the contribution is the accounting, not the comparison.

**NARROW-3.** Someone has connected government mortgage guarantees to capital regulation's
treatment. Then option 1's attachment is weakened but the measurement survives.

## What would leave it intact

**INTACT.** No one has expressed the federal guarantee as a share of the household or
wage-backed claim stock, and no one has set the US holder leg against a comparator economy on
a common accounting.

## Decision rule, fixed now

- **KILL** → report it, stop, and recommend the regulatory point alone.
- **NARROW-1 or NARROW-2 only** → proceed, with the contribution stated as the narrower
  thing and the ceiling restated.
- **Two or more NARROW conditions together** → treat as close to a kill; the residual
  contribution is probably too thin for five sessions, and say so.
- **INTACT** → proceed as scoped.

**The count of narrowing conditions found is reported whatever it is.** Searches that return
nothing are reported as such rather than taken as evidence of novelty, since absence of a
search hit is weak evidence.

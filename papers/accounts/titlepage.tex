\noindent\textbf{This is the first of two companion papers.} It reports the measurement: what
income services the US claim stock and who holds, guarantees or owes it. The second,
\textit{Ownership, Not Profit Shifting: Why Capital Taxation Cannot Replace Wage Taxes}, uses
these accounts to ask whether the tax system can replace the revenue when income moves from
labour to capital. Each stands alone and neither repeats the other.

\vspace{0.5em}

value originates in a model's assertion: every number in the text and in the tables is generated
from a measured artifact in the replication package.
